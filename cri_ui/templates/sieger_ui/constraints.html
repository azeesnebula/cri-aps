“Over capacity” is not a utilisation. A machine cannot be more than 100% busy. Where you see needs 3× capacity, the plan has been told to put three times more work through that step than it physically has hours for — because the due dates demand it. It is not what will happen on the floor; it is the size of the gap between what has been promised and what exists.

What happens when you press Run

  1. Your constraint is filled first. Solid, no gaps. An hour lost there is an hour the whole factory never gets back.
  2. Parts are timed to arrive just before it needs them. Not earlier, or stock piles up in front of it.
  3. Everything else is arranged around that.

The other machines will look quieter. That is correct.

A laser sitting idle for a day is not waste. Running it flat out just to look busy makes parts nobody needs yet, and they sit as stock.

If everything got busier after a run, something is wrong — tell us.

Then what

Open the plan board and find your constraint. Its row should be one solid bar. The delivery dates on work orders are now built from that schedule.
